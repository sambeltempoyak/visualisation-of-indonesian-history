## Open questions
- Papua/Onin flow (slide 16) is generic: Bosma says slaves were carried from New Guinea but gives no numbers or destination; Raben says Papuan slaves did not travel west in large numbers. Flow to Tidore is illustrative and should be checked.
- Slide 7 Bali and slide 13 Mascarene: French carriage from Bali; Allen has detail Ejak could read.
- Pre-1500 bondage was not sourced; slide 1 starts with c. 1511.
- Slide 12 numbers are contested; Sutherland's critique not read in full.
- Flow routes and coordinates are schematic, not documented routes.
