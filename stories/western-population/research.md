# Research notes: western-population

## How I worked
Only the cloud workspace (web search and fetch). Library PDFs on Ejak's computer were not used. Many publisher sites and the OAPEN and NIDI hosts block direct download here, so texts were read through the fetch tool, which returns summaries of the page text; page numbers below are as that tool reported them from the printed pages. A few fetches were refused for permission, so some items below are unchecked.

## Disagreements
- 1930 total: 240,417 (NIDI Table 3.1), 240,162 (age tables), about 243,000 with ship crews, 246,000 (Van Imhoff & Beets, JPR p. 53), "240,000" (rounded elsewhere).
- 1942 population: 290,000 (NIOD), 294,000 or 305,000 (NIDI).
- Camp deaths: 10,580 to 16,800 (NIOD range); Bosma gives percentages 10% and 23%. NIOD gives POW deaths 19.4%.
- Outside camps: 120,000–200,000 vs Bosma's "half interned".
- Bersiap: 3,500 (NIDI model) vs 25,000–30,000 casualties (Van den Berge citing Frederick). Different measures possibly.
- Departures: 254,000 (1946–67) vs almost 300,000 (1945–62) vs 312,500 (1950s–60s incl. Moluccans).

## Unverified or not found
- Europeans by census before 1920 (1860, 1880, 1900, 1905): none verified. Java and Madura figures of 44,000 (1880), 55,000 (1890), 72,000 (1900), 133,000 (1920) and 189,000 (1930) appear on Wikipedia citing Wiseman (ASAA, 2000); I could not check Wiseman and left them out. 189,000 would be about 79% of 240,000, consistent with the 80% claim.
- 1930 counts for Bandung, Surabaya, Semarang, Malang, Medan city: not found.
- Number leaving in 1957–58 specifically: not found. Total remaining in Indonesia after 1958: not found. The "West New Guinea dispute" as cause of 5 Dec 1957 comes from the brief; fetched sources only describe the event.
- Dutch residents leaving New Guinea 1962–63; current Western population of Indonesia: not found.
- Ricklefs 2008 and Stoler 1985 not read (Stoler cited as context only on slide 7). Cultivation System not covered, no verified source.
- Volkstelling 1930 vol. VI itself not opened (Delpher/KITLV); figures via secondary sources. Primary entry has no publisher or print year.
- Locher-Scholten p. 86 passage: chapter not identified. Bosma 2012 Jones page numbers inferred from running headers.
- Captain & Sinke 398-camp figure: timing within 1945–46 not checked.
- NIDI c. 1860 male-female figure: group definition unclear.
- Peer-review status not verified for Leidschrift, Indonesia (1967), AUP Captain & Sinke; marked academic only.
- Van Imhoff & Beets JPR end page and DOI not verified.
