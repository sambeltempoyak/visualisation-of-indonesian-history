Built by Ejak (Reza Arlianda), PhD researcher at the Bartlett School of Architecture, UCL, with Claude. Every claim on the map is sourced: each card names its sources and says how certain the claim is. Ethnic-group areas and administrative boundaries are generalised and approximate, and say so.
